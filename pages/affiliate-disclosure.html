Affiliate and Sponsorship Disclosure

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version: Move Out Japan is an independent, English-language move-out support service. We do not currently publish affiliate links or paid placements on this site. If that changes, the link or placement will be labelled clearly on the page where it appears, and this page will explain the policy. A fee will never buy a positive recommendation.

This page explains how Move Out Japan handles commercial relationships in its guides, service pages and partner enquiries. It applies to any affiliate link, referral link, sponsored article or paid placement that appears on this site.

1. What this page covers

This disclosure covers:

  • affiliate and referral links in articles, guides and service pages;
  • sponsored or paid content, including paid placements and paid link insertions if we ever accept them;
  • partnership enquiries and how a commercial relationship is agreed;
  • how we keep editorial recommendations independent of any fee.

It does not cover the handling of your personal data. That is explained in our Privacy Notice.

2. Affiliate and referral links

An affiliate or referral link is a link that may earn Move Out Japan a fee or other benefit if you click it and then buy or use a service. The price you pay should not change because of the link, but the provider’s own terms always apply.

If we use an affiliate or referral link, we will:

  • disclose it on the page where the link appears, near the link or near the top of the page;
  • use plain language, not a code word or an icon that readers may not understand;
  • make the disclosure visible in size and colour, not hidden in small print;
  • keep the recommendation independent — a link is not a promise that the service is right for you;
  • avoid reproducing commission rates or programme terms unless the provider has approved that wording.

3. Sponsored content and paid placements

Sponsored content is content that a partner pays for or otherwise supports. If we publish it, we will label it clearly as sponsored or paid, near the top of the page. A paid placement does not buy a positive conclusion: if we cannot recommend the service on its merits, we decline the placement.

We do not sell link insertions. We may consider a genuine content collaboration when the topic is useful to foreign residents and the author can support the claims with sources. Any paid or sponsored element is labelled, and editorial control stays with the site that publishes it.

The Consumer Affairs Agency’s guidance on stealth marketing says an affiliate site should make its commercial relationship clear and visible, and that paid or requested posts can amount to “business representation” and need to be labelled. We treat those principles as the minimum. See the agency’s stealth-marketing Q&A (Japanese) and English site.

4. Editorial independence

Our guides are written for foreign residents moving out of, or settling into, a Japanese home. We link to official sources where an answer depends on law or public guidance, and we try to separate fact from opinion. A commercial relationship does not change that approach.

In practice:

  • we do not accept a placement on condition that it receives a positive review;
  • we do not let a partner approve or suppress criticism of its own service;
  • we do not present a paid relationship as independent editorial;
  • we correct factual errors and date substantive changes;
  • we decline work that would require us to make a claim we cannot support.

5. How we choose partners

We look for relevance to foreign residents, clear customer terms, sensible data handling, a complaints route, and a willingness to be disclosed. We also check that the service can actually be delivered in the customer’s area and language. We confirm coverage per job rather than claiming nationwide service.

Our partners page explains the models we consider and what to include in an enquiry. The longer guide, Partner With Move Out Japan, sets out the assessment and publishing rules in more detail.

6. Current status

As of 8 November 2026, this site does not publish affiliate links or paid placements. The external links in our guides point to official sources, public programme pages and services we cite for research or context. A link to a public programme page is not a partnership, an endorsement or a claim that we have joined the programme.

If we add an affiliate link or paid placement, we will update this page and label the relevant page. We will not quietly add a commercial link to an existing article and leave the disclosure only in the footer.

7. What we do not do

  • we do not invent partners, testimonials, approvals or business contacts;
  • we do not publish commission rates or unapproved offers;
  • we do not sell link insertions or disguise paid links as editorial;
  • we do not guarantee a deposit refund, visa outcome, price or response time;
  • we do not share quote-request data with a partner without asking you first.
