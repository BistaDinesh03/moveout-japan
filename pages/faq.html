Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about moving out and furniture disposal in Japan.

Can foreigners use this service?+

Absolutely! Move Out Japan is specifically designed for international students, foreign workers, and expats living in Japan. All communication is available in English, and we understand the unique challenges foreigners face when moving out in Japan.

Is furniture disposal legal?+

Yes, 100% compliant. We follow Japan's Home Appliance Recycling Law and all local waste management regulations. All items are disposed through certified recycling channels.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept PayPay, bank transfer, LINE Pay, and cash on pickup. Payment is made after service completion for your peace of mind. We provide receipts in English and Japanese.

What areas do you cover?+

We cover major cities including Fukuoka, Saga, Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya, Kyoto, Yokohama, Saitama, Chiba, Kawasaki, Sapporo, Kobe, Hiroshima, and Sendai. If you're unsure, contact us!

How quickly can I schedule pickup?+

We typically respond with a quote within 24 hours. Once confirmed, we can schedule pickup within 2-7 days. For urgent situations, contact us on LINE for faster arrangements.

Do you help with apartment checkout?+

While we don't attend the official inspection, we ensure your apartment is completely cleared of unwanted items. This significantly increases your chances of getting your full deposit back.

What items can you NOT take?+

We cannot handle hazardous materials, chemicals, gasoline, paint, or items containing asbestos. Standard household furniture, electronics, and appliances are all fine.

Do I need to be present?+

Yes, someone needs to be present to grant access. If you cannot be there, a trusted friend, colleague, or your real estate agent can be present instead.
