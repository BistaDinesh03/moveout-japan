How Your Quote Works Out

We don't publish fixed prices, because we can't honestly quote a fridge, a sixth-floor walk-up, and a full apartment clear-out the same way. You send us what you've got, and we price that specific job.

What your quote depends on

The items themselves

Type, size, condition, and quantity. Appliances such as fridges, washing machines, TVs, and air conditioners follow the Home Appliance Recycling Law route, which carries its own recycling handling — very different from a desk or a bookshelf.

Building & access

Floor level, stairs or elevator, distance from the room to the truck, and your building's rules — many Japanese apartments require booking a moving slot or using a designated loading area.

Where and when

Your location in Japan and your preferred timing both affect the route and schedule. Coverage for your address is confirmed before you commit to anything.

What we check before you book

  • The full total — you see the complete price for your job before any work is scheduled.
  • What's included — labour, carrying, transport, and the disposal route for each item, spelled out in English.
  • Whether we can reach you — coverage for your area is confirmed up front, not after you've committed.
  • Timing that actually works — scheduling depends on job size, location, and your building's rules, so we agree it with you directly.
  • How to pay — payment options are confirmed with your booking, in writing, before the work starts.
💡 Clearing a whole apartment? Send photos and a rough list of what's staying and what's going — we'll price it as one job rather than item by item.
