Yes, 100% compliant. We follow Japan's Home Appliance Recycling Law and all local waste management regulations. All items are disposed through certified recycling channels. You receive proper documentation if needed for your apartment checkout.

We accept PayPay, bank transfer, LINE Pay, and cash on pickup. Payment is made after service completion for your peace of mind. We provide receipts in English and Japanese.
