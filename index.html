The Problem

Moving out in Japan gets stressful fast — especially when you don't understand the recycling system.

Losing Your Deposit

If appliances are left in your apartment, landlords may deduct expensive removal fees from your deposit (敷金). Some students lose ¥30,000–¥60,000.

Language Barrier

City office websites, recycling forms, and phone calls are confusing even for Japanese people. You shouldn't struggle alone.

Legal Requirements

TVs, fridges, washing machines, and air conditioners require proper recycling under Japan's Home Appliance Recycling Law (家電リサイクル法).
