Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners use this moving out service?+

Absolutely! Move Out Japan is specifically designed for international students, foreign workers, and expats living in Japan. All communication is available in English, and we understand the unique challenges foreigners face when moving out in Japan.

Is furniture disposal in Japan legal through your service?+

We plan every job around Japan's waste rules: designated large items go through your municipality's pickup procedure, and appliances go through the Home Appliance Recycling Law process. If something can't be handled for your building, we tell you before booking.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We confirm the payment options with you when your booking is confirmed, so you know the total and how to pay before the work starts.

How quickly can I schedule furniture disposal?+

Timing depends on your location, the size of the job, and building access rules. Send us your details and we'll come back with the next steps. For urgent situations, contact us on LINE.

Do you help with apartment checkout inspection?+

We don't attend the official inspection, but we clear your apartment of unwanted items so the space is ready for it. Deposit outcomes are decided by your landlord or management company under your contract.
