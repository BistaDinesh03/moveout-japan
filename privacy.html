Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 October 2026

The short version: if you send us a quote request, we use what you give us — your name, email, area in Japan, and what you want removed — for one purpose only: to reply with a price. This website has no database, no advertising trackers, and no analytics. You can ask us to delete your message at any time.

1. Who we are

Move Out Japan is an English-language furniture disposal and move-out support service operating in Japan. This notice covers moveoutjapan.pages.dev.

Contact for anything in this notice: bistadinesh642@gmail.com, or message us on LINE at @704xslsr.

2. What we collect

Our quote form asks for four things:

  • Your name
  • Your email address — so we can reply to you
  • Your area in Japan — a city/region from a fixed list
  • A description of the items you want removed

The form also records the date and time of your submission in Japan time, which helps us handle requests in the order they arrive.

We deliberately do not ask for a phone number, postal address, payment details, nationality, or any other sensitive information. Please don't put anything sensitive in the free-text description.

3. How it is used, and on what basis

We use this information only to prepare and send you a quote and to answer your follow-up questions. That is the whole purpose; we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

Because the information is used solely to respond to the request you made, we treat this as handling your enquiry rather than as marketing consent. We do not add you to any mailing list, and nothing on this site subscribes you to anything.

4. Where it goes (the actual data flow)

This site is static. There is no server-side application and no database. When you press send:

  1. Your browser sends the details directly to EmailJS (emailjs.com), a third-party email delivery service;
  2. EmailJS delivers the message to our email inbox (Gmail); and
  3. Optionally, EmailJS sends a confirmation copy to the address you provided.

Companies involved in simply delivering the page to you:

  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery (moveoutjapan.pages.dev).
  • Google Fonts — the fonts on this site load from Google, so your IP address is transmitted to Google when a page loads.
  • EmailJS — message delivery, as described above.

This site itself sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and stores nothing in your browser (no local storage, no session storage, no tracking pixels).

5. How long we keep it

Because the website has no database, your submission is not retained by the site at all — it is delivered to our inbox and kept there as part of our correspondence.

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as we need it to handle your request and our ordinary business records, then delete it. If you would like your message deleted sooner, email us and we will remove it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Don't use the form. You can contact us entirely via LINE (@704xslsr) or email instead — no form is required.
  • Ask what we hold about your enquiry.
  • Ask us to delete it, and we will.
  • Ask us to correct it.

To exercise any of these, email bistadinesh642@gmail.com. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

7. Third-party services

EmailJS, Cloudflare and Google each process information under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. We encourage you to review them:

  • EmailJS privacy policy
  • Cloudflare privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

8. Children

This service is aimed at adults arranging a move or a disposal. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this notice

If we change what this site collects or how it is handled, we will update this page and revise the date above. If we start using information for anything beyond answering your enquiry, we will ask you first.
